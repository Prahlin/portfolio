The Way I Work

My Workflow

This page is personal, but it stays practical. Every story beat ties back to a skill, artifact, or production outcome.

01

Research

  1. A: Discuss
  2. B: Brainstorm
  3. C: Industry Research

Banking customers are not Video Streamers, Global users not American ones. That's why I conduct comprehensive, theoretical Industry & Product Research before the practical work of UI/UX, Front-End, Back-End, Build & Release planning even begins.

Evidence: UI/UX philosophy, visual hierarchy studies, shipped mobile screens.

02

Plan

  1. A: Wireframing
  2. B: Mockups
  3. C: Scaffold

Cross-platform or Platform-specific? Web version or App-only? UI-focused or Back-end heavy?

I help make the difficult decisions about your unique product.

Evidence: Alla Vostra, Cinerific, Credit King.

03

Produce

  1. A: Build
  2. B: Testing
  3. C: Deployment

Although the nitty-gritty of every project is different, some systematization is paramount; that's why I always, without exception, begin every session with read-project briefings, commit changes in an organized manner, track real-time snapshots of project changes, and generate end-of-day worklogs at all times.

Evidence: Vercel API, Stripe, PayPal, Postmark.

04

Analyze

  1. A: Handoff
  2. B: Upkeep

Creation without learning is useless. By studying & analyzing diffs while the project is growing, I ensure that I'm in the loop, and deeply comprehend, every coding change made to the project in real time.

Evidence: 100+ logs across active projects.
